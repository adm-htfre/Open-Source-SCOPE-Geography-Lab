# Term 2 Geography lessons

Bilingual (English / मराठी) teaching material for SCOPE Grades 8–10, built from the **SCOPE Weekly Curriculum Map 2026-27** (Geography column) for the first three weeks of Term 2.

| Week of | Grade 8 | Grade 9 | Grade 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 23 Nov | Land Use | International Date Line | Population (India & Brazil) |
| 30 Nov | Land Use (cont.) | Introduction to Economics | Human Settlements |
| 7 Dec | Population | Introduction to Economics (cont.) | Human Settlements (cont.) |

Open `lessons/index.html`. Each lesson has slides to project (← → keys), one activity, a three-question check and teacher notes. Links like `index.html#g9-date-line-s3` open a lesson at a given slide.

**Sources:** Census of India 2011 (India, Maharashtra, Sangli); World Bank World Development Indicators (India and Brazil, 2023–2025). Chapter names follow the curriculum map; teachers should check terms and figures against the Balbharati textbooks.

**Editing:** content lives in `lessons/lessons.tpl.html` (the `LESSONS` list). Map outlines are in `lessons/shapes.json`; the build replaces `/*SHAPES*/` in the template with it.
